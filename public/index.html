My story

Sofia

Owner of ADAM'S Cafeteria & Conditorei

«Gutes Essen macht Menschen glücklich.»

Good food makes people happy.

My name is Sofia. I was born in Horgen, my family comes from Morocco, and my life has been made of journeys and changes of work, some of them sudden. This is my story.

  1. 01Horgen · Morocco

    The roots

    My parents came to Switzerland from Morocco before I was born. I was born and grew up in Horgen.

    My love of cooking was handed down to me by my mother. Meanwhile I trained in foreign languages, which have stayed with me as another great passion to this day.

  2. 02Switzerland

    Quality and engines

    I trained as a quality specialist and worked in Switzerland for a well-known engineering company: workshops, inspections, procedures, attention to every detail.

    That job taught me a great deal. But my passion was still the kitchen.

  3. 03Italy

    Front of house and kitchen

    Then came Italy. That is where I started working in restaurants, both front of house and in the kitchen: serving, listening to guests, learning the rhythm of a service.

  4. 04Sweden

    Something of my own

    In Sweden I refined my cooking skills and learned the local techniques.

    That is where the plan to create something of my own was born. First Nonna Maria, an Italian food truck; then a cooking school, where I began to teach what I had learned.

  5. 05Back in Switzerland

    Back in the kitchen

    Finally I came back to Switzerland, and back to the kitchen. It was not always easy: starting over, finding my place again, sometimes taking one step back to take two forward.

  6. 06Horgen · Dorfgasse

    The decision

    After a long time I made the decision: to open something of my own. That is how ADAM'S was born, in Horgen, where it all began.

    A café, a Conditorei, a kitchen and a little room for art. There is a piece of every stage of this journey in it.

With my daughters

Everything here is prepared with a lot of love, and with the precious help of my daughters. Thank you for your help, your support and for always being by my side. I am incredibly proud of you.

[Their names and what each of them does at the café.]

Art meets cuisine

For me, good food and art belong together. That is why paintings hang on our walls and why we cook for openings and events, like the Sensorik-Apéro at Kunstraum Kesselhaus in September 2026, with homemade specialities, olive oil and wine.

Regional ingredients.Real passion.Special moments.

Sofia
